# Mama Put – Eat good food

Mama Put is a Freetown cookery that serves workers, students and families who want hot, home-style Salone plates such as cassava leaf, potato leaf, jollof and fufu with okra, and who already order on WhatsApp and pay cash on delivery. The page puts the name, the tagline "Eat good food", a Krio line ("Wetin yu want chop?") and WhatsApp and Call buttons at the top and in a sticky bottom bar, with a photo menu of eight dishes priced in Leones (NLe), each with its own Order button, then opening hours, a landmark location near the Eastern Police Clock Tower and cash-on-delivery steps. The colours come from the food itself (palm-oil red, turmeric gold and cassava-leaf green) with strong contrast, 18 px text, large tap targets and alt text on every photo. To stay fast on slow Salone networks, the page is plain HTML and CSS with no framework or web fonts, the logo is a tiny inline SVG and the eight dish photos are cropped and compressed WebP files (about 6–16 KB each), so the whole page is about 104 KB, far under the 500 KB limit. A small script also shows whether the shop is open right now.
