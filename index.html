How to order and pay

Cash on delivery

Pay in cash when your food reaches you. Delivery fee depends on where you are, so ask us on WhatsApp.

  1. Send us a message on WhatsApp. Tok to wi na WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us your dish and how many plates. We confirm the price in NLe.
  3. We bring it to you and you pay cash. Wi go bring am gi yu.
